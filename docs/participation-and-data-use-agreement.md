# NAPE AICE 2026 Hackathon — Participation and Data Use Agreement

**Status: DRAFT FOR ORGANISER AND LEGAL APPROVAL — NOT YET EFFECTIVE**

**Event:** NAPE AICE 2026 Hackathon, 7–8 November 2026, Eko Hotel & Suites, Lagos  
**Theme:** Unlocking Dormant Value

This agreement is intended to be accepted by each participant **before receiving a team laptop or access to challenge materials**. The final approved version, rather than this draft, governs participation. The organiser must confirm the legal entity, governing law, contact details and dataset permissions before issue.

## 1. Participation and acknowledgement

By accepting the final agreement, I confirm that I am participating in the NAPE AICE 2026 Hackathon, will follow the published [Competition Rules](competition-rules.md), and will use the approved challenge materials only as permitted. Each participant must acknowledge the agreement individually; a team leader's acceptance does not replace individual acceptance.

## 2. Approved data and access

- Organisers will provide approved challenge-specific datasets preloaded on team laptops at the event. Participants do not need to download the datasets.
- The dataset is curated from publicly released Volve Field material. Public availability **does not automatically establish unrestricted copying, redistribution, commercial use or third-party processing rights**.
- Participants may use the approved materials solely to develop, test and demonstrate their assigned hackathon solution, subject to the source dataset's applicable terms and any additional written organiser instructions.
- Access to any additional dataset, external source or restricted material requires prior written organiser approval.

## 3. Handling and protection of data

Participants must:
- Keep supplied files and access credentials within their team and authorised event environment.
- Not publish, sell, share or redistribute the raw supplied datasets or restricted organiser materials.
- Not attempt to access withheld ground truth, answer keys, hidden evaluation questions, scoring scripts, other teams' private work or organiser-only folders.
- Not upload supplied source documents, raw files or identifiable sensitive content to third-party AI services, cloud storage, external APIs or personal devices **unless the organisers expressly approve that processing route** and confirm it is compatible with the source terms.
- Promptly notify the organisers of any accidental disclosure, loss of a laptop, credential exposure or suspected unauthorised access.
- Return organiser-provided laptops and remove any unauthorised copies as directed after the event, subject to an approved retention or showcase arrangement.

## 4. Tools and AI use

Open-source tools, public pretrained models and AI coding assistants may be used under the approved Competition Rules. External services or commercial APIs are permitted **only if expressly approved for the relevant use**. Participants must disclose material third-party models, datasets, services and generated content in their submissions. AI-generated outputs remain subject to verification, evidence and attribution requirements.

## 5. Submissions, attribution and intellectual property

- Each team must submit its required machine-readable outputs **and a working codebase, notebook or application**, plus a README and demonstration, as specified in the approved challenge brief.
- Participants retain ownership of original work they create **unless a separate, expressly agreed written arrangement provides otherwise**. This agreement does not transfer ownership of participant-created intellectual property to NAPE, sponsors or organisers.
- Participants must respect licences and rights in source data, third-party libraries, pretrained models and other materials; they may not claim ownership of the supplied Volve source material.
- Teams grant the organisers a **non-exclusive, royalty-free permission** to access, run, reproduce internally and assess submitted materials for hackathon administration, judging, audit and verification. **Any public release, commercial use or transfer of submitted code or solutions requires separate permission or an approved release licence.**
- Attribution to the underlying Volve data owner and any other required notices must follow the approved dataset licence and organiser guidance. **Exact attribution wording: [TO CONFIRM].**

## 6. Publicity, recordings and personal information

Participation does not by itself grant unrestricted permission to use a participant's image, voice or personal story in publicity. Any optional photography, filming, testimonial or promotional consent should be collected separately, with an appropriate privacy notice. Registration and judging information will be handled according to the event's approved privacy notice **[LINK / CONTROLLER DETAILS TO CONFIRM]**.

## 7. Fairness, safety and enforcement

Participants must follow venue safety directions, respect other teams and organisers, avoid harassment, and comply with event rules. The organisers may investigate suspected breaches and, following a fair review, impose proportionate sanctions up to disqualification. Serious safety or security incidents may require immediate restriction of access.

## 8. Final legal provisions — approval required

Before publication, the organisers must approve: the contracting organiser/legal entity; contact for incidents and privacy; applicable law and dispute process; eligibility and any age/guardian requirements; dataset licence and redistribution permissions; external API/data-processing rules; liability language; retention/deletion period; and any sponsor-specific IP or showcase terms. **No unapproved legal provision should be inferred from this draft.**
